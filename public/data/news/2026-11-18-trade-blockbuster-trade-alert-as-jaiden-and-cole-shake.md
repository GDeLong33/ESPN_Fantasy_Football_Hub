Let me tell you something right now... I am FLABBERGASTED! I am looking at the transaction wire in this league, and my jaw has completely hit the FLOOR!

Cole, sitting down at 2-8 with the North Atlantic 1912 Swim Team, decided he needed an entire fleet of lifeboats. And what did he do? He hauled in Jayden Daniels, Drake London, AND Tony Pollard! That is three bonafide starters! We are talking about 136 fantasy points from Daniels, 138.5 from London, and 140.4 from Pollard. Cole saw his season sinking and grabbed three solid oars to row back to respectability!

BUT AT WHAT COST?!

Let me ask you this: how on earth do you hand BIJAN ROBINSON to a 7-3 powerhouse?! Jaiden is already dominating with No Diddy Giddy, and now you gift-wrap him Bijan—who already boasts 197.7 points on the year—plus Emeka Egbuka?! Are you SERIOUS?!

Now, do not get me wrong. I understand what Cole is doing. When you are 2-8, you cannot afford to have all your eggs in one basket. Even Austin is looking at Cole's desperation and thinking, hey, at least somebody is fighting down there! Cole got depth. Cole got production across three different starting slots.

HOWEVER... stars win championships! Jaiden gave up depth, sure, but he walked away with the single most dangerous weapon in this entire deal to cement his playoff juggernaut.

Cole made a frantic move to save his sinking ship... but Jaiden just pulled off absolute daylight robbery! UNBELIEVABLE!
